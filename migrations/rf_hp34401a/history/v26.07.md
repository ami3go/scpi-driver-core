# History — v26.07

## Added

- RFDS-008 structured evidence engine (`hp34401a_dmm/evidence.py`): every public keyword call
  is recorded with correlated arguments, duration, result/failure, and the literal SCPI
  commands/responses it caused on whichever transport (VISA, RS-232, Prologix, or the
  simulator) carried it, tagged per DMM alias, finalized into a SHA-256-hashed manifest under
  `results/session/rf_hp34401a/`.
- `Export Diagnostic Bundle` keyword (109th public keyword) — zips the current evidence run.
- `docs/logging_and_evidence.md`, `guide/evidence_and_diagnostics.md`,
  `schemas/evidence/*.schema.json`, `scripts/validate_evidence.{py,sh,bat,ps1}`,
  `tests/evidence/test_evidence.py`.

## Unchanged

- SCPI behavior, measurement logic, configuration schemas, capability identifiers, and every
  existing public keyword's signature and return value.
- The RFDS-019 real-hardware (`tests/hil/`) and protocol-vector (`tests/conformance/`)
  conformance suites remain the coverage authority; this release only added the one new
  keyword's entries to their inventories and regenerated their derived artifacts.
